About HITAM

Hyderabad Institute of Technology and Management (HITAM) is one of Telangana's premier autonomous engineering institutions dedicated to academic excellence, innovation, research and industry-oriented education. Established with the vision of producing globally competent engineers, HITAM provides modern laboratories, experienced faculty, industry collaborations, research opportunities and an excellent learning environment. Through outcome-based education, advanced technology, entrepreneurship initiatives and strong placement support, HITAM prepares students for successful careers in engineering, higher education and innovation.

About Project

The HITAM Smart AI Doubt Resolution Portal is an intelligent academic support platform that allows students to submit academic doubts, receive instant AI-generated answers powered by Google Gemini, and obtain faculty-verified solutions through one centralized system. The platform reduces faculty workload, improves student learning, supports 24×7 doubt clarification, and provides a modern digital learning experience for engineering education.
