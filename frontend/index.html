About HITAM

Hyderabad Institute of Technology and Management (HITAM) is an autonomous engineering institution committed to academic excellence, innovation, research, and industry-oriented learning. HITAM provides quality education through experienced faculty, modern laboratories, advanced infrastructure, and strong industry collaborations that prepare students for successful careers.

About the Project

The HITAM Smart AI Doubt Resolution Portal is an intelligent web application developed to simplify academic doubt solving. Students can register, securely log in, submit academic doubts, receive AI-generated answers, and obtain faculty-verified solutions through one centralized platform. The portal improves learning efficiency while reducing faculty workload.
